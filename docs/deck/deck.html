VITALIS3 / 7

How it decides

Transparent rules that know what is normal for you.

1 · Safe limits

Every limit cites its source (NASA standards, clinical guidelines) and adapts to the mission, e.g. radiation for Mars vs the ISS.

2 · Your personal baseline

A 14-day average for each person catches "normal on paper, abnormal for you", and 3-day trends.

3 · 11 combination rules

Eye & head pressure (SANS), solar storm, mood & isolation, bone loss, sleep debt, infection, kidney stones and more, each linked to a NASA risk.

Tap an organ: why it was flagged, what to do, and "the doctors on Earth can answer in ~24 min at the earliest, start these steps now".

Works with no internet · Helps on Earth too

Saved on the device first. Sent to Earth when a link exists.

Fully offline

Installs as an app on phone, tablet and laptop. All checks run on the device; uploads wait in a queue and send themselves when a link returns, like NASA's store-and-forward networking.

Benefits on Earth

Remote clinics, polar stations, ships and disaster zones: anywhere with no doctor nearby and a slow or missing connection.
