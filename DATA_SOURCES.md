# Data & sources used in VITALIS

This file lists everything VITALIS is built on: what was used, where it came from, where it appears in the project, and under what terms. Each item is marked:

- **✅ Real data**: measured data downloaded from NASA and used as-is (only reformatted).
- **📘 Resource**: official NASA / CSA information used to design features, rules and text.
- **📏 Published value**: a number taken from a NASA standard or a scientific paper.
- **🧪 Made up**: generated by VITALIS for the demo, clearly not real.

---

## 1. Official NASA Space Apps 2026 resources (challenge resources)

All five resources given for the challenge *Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions* are used. In the app they are listed on **About & Sources** (`src/data/resources.ts`).

| # | Resource | Type | How VITALIS uses it | Where in the project | Terms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | NASA, [5 Hazards of Human Spaceflight](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/) | 📘 Resource | Every measurement, alert, guide and body area is tagged with its hazard (radiation, isolation, distance, gravity, hostile environment). "Distance from Earth" drives the delay-aware escalation ("help is ~24 min away, start now"). | `src/data/hazards.ts`, `src/data/indicators.ts`, `src/engine/escalation.ts`, onboarding, dashboard hazard strip | NASA web content, public; NASA media usage guidelines (nasa.gov) |
| 2 | NASA, [Risks of Human Spaceflight](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/) | 📘 Resource | Each body area maps to its HRP risk (eye/head pressure (SANS), heart, bone loss, muscle loss, behaviour, immune, nutrition, kidney stones, dust). The 11 combination rules target these risks. | `src/data/systems.ts`, `src/engine/triage.ts`, body map panels | as above |
| 3 | NASA, [Ongoing HRP Research](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/ongoing-hrp-research/) | 📘 Resource | Countermeasure guides (exercise, sleep lighting, CO₂ headaches, eye checks, workload); reaction-speed test and personal baselines follow how in-flight studies track crews. Linked from 10 guides. | `src/data/guidance.ts`, `src/engine/pvt.ts`, `src/engine/baseline.ts` | as above |
| 4 | CSA, [Monitoring Radiation for Astronaut Health](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/news/articles/2026/2026-02-26-monitoring-radiation-astronaut-health.asp) | 📘 Resource | Daily dosimeter reading in every check-in, live dose-rate alarm, solar-storm shelter steps, Radiation exposure panel. | `src/engine/thresholds.ts` (radiation), `src/engine/radiation.ts`, `src/features/radiation/` | CSA website terms and conditions (asc-csa.gc.ca) |
| 5 | CSA, [Why Do We Conduct Science Experiments in Space?](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/iss/science/why-do-we-conduct-science-experiments-in-space.asp) | 📘 Resource | "Benefits on Earth" (remote care, polar stations, ships, disasters, home monitoring). | `src/data/resources.ts`, About page, README | as above |

## 5. Made-up (synthetic) data: clearly not real

| What | Why it is made up | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Demo crew health readings (heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen, sleep, mood, weight, exercise, reaction tests, journal) | Individual astronaut medical records are private and not published by NASA | `src/data/seed.ts` (fixed random seed, same story every time) |
| Demo crew names and mission "ARES-1" | Fictional characters | `src/data/seed.ts` |
| Flight-surgeon replies in local/demo mode | No real ground team in demo mode (with accounts, replies come from real people) | `src/features/ground/` |
| Sensor simulator and its faults | Lets you test alarms without hardware | `src/devices/simulator.ts` |

## 7. Use of SI tools

The code, documentation and NASA data-processing scripts (`scripts/nasa-data/`) were generated with the help of an SI coding assistant, following the team's instructions; the team designed the features, tested the app and directed every fix. The NASA radiation values themselves are real measurements, only reformatted by these scripts. Screenshots are real captures of the app (not SI-generated).

## 8. Not used

- No real astronaut medical records (none are public).
- No NASA logos or insignia: VITALIS uses its own logo.
- The NASA Life Sciences Data Archive and Open Science Data Repository were not used. Their data is mostly per-study and restricted, and does not fit daily self-monitoring.
